## Background

The College of Religious Studies is the first institution of religious studies in Thailand and operates as an academic unit under Mahidol University. It was officially established on 1 January 1999 during the tenure of Professor Arthit Urairong (note: if you want the official English name adjusted, I can refine this) as President of Mahidol University. The College evolved from the Religious Studies Center Project at Mahidol University, which was jointly founded in 1997 by Mahidol University and Dr. Arawan Khunwisal, President of the Namthong Khunwisal Foundation, under the Office of the President.

The College is located in the Namthong Khunwisal Building, situated opposite the Faculty of Veterinary Science at Mahidol University, Salaya Campus. The five-storey building is designed to be open and airy. Each floor provides informal discussion areas adjacent to landscaped flower gardens. At the center of the building is a courtyard garden with benches shaded by trees, while the surrounding area is enriched with large trees that create a calm and pleasant academic environment.

## Teaching and Learning Approach

Religious studies at the College are conducted through a combination of classroom instruction with faculty members, small-group discussions, and independent research. Students are encouraged to seek knowledge both in the library and within communities of various religious traditions, through individual inquiry as well as group-based activities.

In addition to courses in religious studies, students have opportunities to enroll in general education courses offered by the University, as well as other subjects that support professional development and effective participation in society in the 21st century, such as science, computer studies, and English.
